Synthetic Benchmark Dataset Information

Data architecture, generation parameters, and engineering boundaries

1. Scope and Scale

The benchmark dataset comprises exactly 50 component records (IDs: CB001 through CB050) spanning 10 distinct automotive subsystems: Door Panel, Dashboard Frame, Control Arm, Battery Bracket, Motor Housing, Subframe, Bumper Beam, Hood Outer, Brake Caliper, and Steering Knuckle.

2. Material Representation (20% Per Class)

Fleet records are distributed equally across 5 major structural material categories (10 records each): Steel, Aluminum, Engineering Polymers / Plastic, Magnesium Alloys, and Cast Iron.

3. Currency & Cost Calibration Policy

All baseline monetary values in data/components.csv are recorded natively in United States Dollars (USD). Converted Indian Rupee (INR) displays in the interface utilize a centralized, configurable estimation rate (USD_TO_INR_RATE) and are marked as estimates. All variance percentages are mathematically computed on base USD amounts, guaranteeing that rate adjustments never alter percentage metrics.

4. Engineering Transparency & Non-OEM Disclaimer

This dataset is synthetic and configured specifically for benchmarking algorithms, RAG document retrieval demonstrations, and LLM prompt grounding. It does not represent proprietary teardown engineering, confidential OEM CAD models, or certified production tolerances.
